PRIVACY NOTICE

Privacy at the waitlist stage.

Effective September 23, 2026

Who manages PIROKA

PIROKA is managed by X RankFlow Media Group LLC. This notice covers the PIROKA coming-soon website and early access waitlist.

What we collect

If you join the early access list, we collect the email address you submit. Our hosting and infrastructure providers may also process ordinary technical information needed to deliver and secure the website, such as IP address, request metadata, device or browser information, and security logs.

The coming-soon page stores a simple local browser flag to remember that you joined. That flag does not contain your email address.

Why we use it

We use waitlist information to manage early access, send PIROKA launch or product updates, prevent abuse, troubleshoot delivery, and operate the launch infrastructure. We do not use the waitlist email to promise access, availability, or a particular launch date.

Service providers

We may use hosting, infrastructure, email, security, analytics, and other vendors to operate PIROKA. Those providers may process information on our behalf under their own service terms and security controls.

Retention and deletion

We keep waitlist information for as long as reasonably needed for the early access and launch purpose, security, recordkeeping, or legal obligations. You may request deletion using the contact method published by PIROKA or X RankFlow Media Group LLC.

Your choices and rights

You can choose not to join the waitlist. Depending on where you live, privacy law may give you rights to request access, correction, deletion, or other controls over personal information. We will evaluate requests under the law that applies to the request.

Age

PIROKA is intended for adults age 18 and older. Do not submit waitlist information if you are under 18.

Changes

We may update this notice as PIROKA develops. The effective date above indicates the current version.
